How every exercise works

  • Start with the reader. Each exercise begins by using AI to understand what's being asked, and setting your own quality requirements.
  • Think before you prompt. A short step without AI, where you review the data yourself. You'll use it to judge the AI's output.
  • Quick prompts and one critical prompt. Quick prompts are copied as given. The critical prompt you build yourself with RCTF+C.
  • Check, don't trust. Every prompt is followed by check questions. Hints and example prompts are hidden until you need them.
  • One new thing each time. Every exercise adds a new way to use AI, building on the ones before.

Every prompt and dataset in the guides sits in a grey box with a Copy button.

Exercise 4-1

Planning an RFQ Response with AI

Open guide

11:13 to 11:27  |  14 minutes  |  Copilot Chat

Goal: Learn to use AI as a thinking partner, not just a writer: understand a request quickly, let AI ask what it needs, build a structured plan, then have AI critique it and challenge that critique in turn.

Tasks

  1. Use AI to understand what the client's RFQ asks for and what it leaves unclear.
  2. Review internal notes yourself to spot what could clash with the client's expectations.
  3. Let AI ask you questions about your own situation before it suggests anything.
  4. Build an RCTF+C prompt for a response plan with workstreams, owners, dates, risks and client questions.
  5. Have AI critique the plan as the client's evaluator, then challenge which criticisms are assumptions.

New techniques

AI as teacher, interviewer, planner and criticMark your assumptionsCritique the critic

Exercise 4-2

Client Reporting

Open guide

11:27 to 11:37  |  10 minutes  |  Copilot Chat

Goal: Learn to turn rough site notes into an accurate client update, where every fact traces to a source and the wording stays true to what actually happened.

Tasks

  1. Use AI to list what the client wants and suggest quality requirements for the update.
  2. Review your own notes for gaps, inconsistencies and loose ends.
  3. Let AI question your notes before it writes anything.
  4. Build an RCTF+C prompt to draft the update, then check every sentence against your notes.
  5. Have AI read the update as the client, then fix what they would question.

New techniques

Quality requirements from the reader's briefAsk before writingKeep wording true to the source

Exercise 4-3

Shift and Site Coordination

Open guide

11:37 to 11:49  |  12 minutes  |  Copilot Chat (optional: Teams, SharePoint dashboard)

Goal: Learn to use AI as a scenario tester, checking what a change would break before approving it, and to communicate one decision clearly to several people.

Tasks

  1. Use AI to work out exactly what two crew members are asking for, and what's missing.
  2. Test each swap request yourself against the roster and site rules.
  3. Ask AI to play out each swap against every rule.
  4. Build an RCTF+C prompt for a decision, an updated roster and a message for each person affected.
  5. Have AI review the decision as the Camp Manager, then verify it on the roster dashboard (optional).

New techniques

AI as scenario testerGive AI the rulesOne decision, several audiences

Exercise 4-4

Procurement Quote Comparison

Open guide

11:49 to 12:01  |  12 minutes  |  Copilot Chat (optional: Excel)

Goal: Learn to turn messy, inconsistent vendor replies into a table you can trust, by giving AI an exact format and clear rules, then build a recommendation step by step on verified output.

Tasks

  1. Use AI to work out what a complete, comparable quote must contain.
  2. Skim six vendor replies and decide your table columns and rules for messy data.
  3. Build an RCTF+C extraction prompt, then spot-check two vendors against their replies.
  4. Chain a follow-up prompt that uses only your verified table to check vendors, shortlist and flag risks.
  5. Check one price conversion yourself, and keep the decision with you.

New techniques

Schema promptingRules for messy dataSpot-check samplingPrompt chaining

Exercise 4-5

HR Pulse Survey Analysis

Open guide

12:01 to 12:15  |  14 minutes  |  Copilot Chat, work account (optional: Word)

Goal: Learn to use AI to analyse opinions rather than facts: keep the analysis proportionate, accurate and anonymous, surface serious concerns, and turn verified results into a management report.

Tasks

  1. Use AI to work out what the HR Manager needs, and set your quality requirements.
  2. Run a "before you paste" check, then skim 30 comments for your own themes and concerns.
  3. Build an RCTF+C prompt for themes with counts, respondent numbers and word-for-word quotes, then verify them.
  4. Ask AI what doesn't fit, and produce a confidential follow-up note for HR.
  5. Write your own prompt for a management report with frequency, sentiment, insights and recommendations.

New techniques

Before you pasteEvidence rulesException promptingFinding, insight, action

Exercise 4-6

Monthly Cost Variance

Open guide

12:15 to 12:25  |  10 minutes  |  Excel with Copilot in Excel, or Copilot Chat

Goal: Learn to use AI to build the tool rather than give the answer: get Excel formulas that work every month, and have AI explain an inherited formula before you rely on it.

Tasks

  1. Use AI to work out what the Finance Manager needs, including the exact flag rule.
  2. Inspect the inherited workbook for formulas versus typed numbers.
  3. Build an RCTF+C prompt that asks for formulas, not results, then test one row by hand.
  4. Check every flag against the rule, and replace typed totals with formulas.
  5. Ask AI to explain the inherited forecast formula. Bonus: let Copilot in Excel make the edits.

New techniques

Ask for the method, not the resultAI as explainerTest one row by hand
